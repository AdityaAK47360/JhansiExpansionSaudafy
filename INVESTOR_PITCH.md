## Five-year profit & cash-flow projection (math shown)
All amounts in ₹.

Year 1
- Revenue R1 = 1,600,000 (from Mauranipur)
- COGS1 = 40% × R1 = 0.40 × 1,600,000 = 640,000
- Fixed1 = 548,000 (see assumptions)
- EBITDA1 = R1 − COGS1 − Fixed1 = 1,600,000 − 640,000 − 548,000 = 412,000
- Depreciation = 100,000 → EBIT1 = 412,000 − 100,000 = 312,000
- Tax (30%) = 0.30 × 312,000 = 93,600
- Net income1 = 312,000 − 93,600 = 218,400
- Cash flow1 = Net + Depreciation = 218,400 + 100,000 = 318,400

Year 2 (25% revenue growth, fixed costs +6%)
- R2 = R1 × 1.25 = 1,600,000 × 1.25 = 2,000,000
- COGS2 = 0.40 × 2,000,000 = 800,000
- Fixed2 = Fixed1 × 1.06 = 548,000 × 1.06 = 580,880
- EBITDA2 = 2,000,000 − 800,000 − 580,880 = 619,120
- EBIT2 = 619,120 − 100,000 = 519,120
- Tax2 = 0.30 × 519,120 = 155,736
- Net2 = 519,120 − 155,736 = 363,384
- Cash2 = 363,384 + 100,000 = 463,384

Year 3
- R3 = R2 × 1.25 = 2,000,000 × 1.25 = 2,500,000
- COGS3 = 0.40 × 2,500,000 = 1,000,000
- Fixed3 = Fixed2 × 1.06 = 580,880 × 1.06 = 615,732.8
- EBITDA3 = 2,500,000 − 1,000,000 − 615,732.8 = 884,267.2
- EBIT3 = 884,267.2 − 100,000 = 784,267.2
- Tax3 = 0.30 × 784,267.2 = 235,280.16
- Net3 = 784,267.2 − 235,280.16 = 548,987.04
- Cash3 = 548,987.04 + 100,000 = 648,987.04

Year 4
- R4 = R3 × 1.25 = 2,500,000 × 1.25 = 3,125,000
- COGS4 = 0.40 × 3,125,000 = 1,250,000
- Fixed4 = Fixed3 × 1.06 = 615,732.8 × 1.06 = 652,676.768
- EBITDA4 = 3,125,000 − 1,250,000 − 652,676.768 = 1,222,323.232
- EBIT4 = 1,222,323.232 − 100,000 = 1,122,323.232
- Tax4 = 0.30 × 1,122,323.232 = 336,696.9696
- Net4 = 1,122,323.232 − 336,696.9696 = 785,626.2624
- Cash4 = 785,626.2624 + 100,000 = 885,626.2624

Year 5
- R5 = R4 × 1.25 = 3,125,000 × 1.25 = 3,906,250
- COGS5 = 0.40 × 3,906,250 = 1,562,500
- Fixed5 = Fixed4 × 1.06 = 652,676.768 × 1.06 = 691,836.37408
- EBITDA5 = 3,906,250 − 1,562,500 − 691,836.37408 = 1,651,913.62592
- EBIT5 = 1,651,913.62592 − 100,000 = 1,551,913.62592
- Tax5 = 0.30 × 1,551,913.62592 = 465,574.087776
- Net5 = 1,551,913.62592 − 465,574.087776 = 1,086,339.538144
- Cash5 = 1,086,339.538144 + 100,000 = 1,186,339.538144

## Aggregate results and investor metrics (based on the above)
- Cash flows Years 1–5:
  - Year1 = 318,400
  - Year2 = 463,384
  - Year3 = 648,987.04
  - Year4 = 885,626.2624
  - Year5 = 1,186,339.538144
- Cumulative undiscounted cash flows (Y1–Y5) = 318,400 + 463,384 + 648,987.04 + 885,626.2624 + 1,186,339.538144 = ₹3,502,736.840544 ≈ ₹3,502,737
- Net undiscounted gain after recovering initial ₹2,500,000 = 3,502,737 − 2,500,000 = ₹1,002,737
- Payback period calculation:
  - Cumulative after Year3 = 318,400 + 463,384 + 648,987.04 = ₹1,430,771.04
  - Remaining to recover = 2,500,000 − 1,430,771.04 = ₹1,069,228.96
  - After Year4 cumulative = 1,430,771.04 + 885,626.2624 = ₹2,316,397.3024
  - Remaining after Year4 = 2,500,000 − 2,316,397.3024 = ₹183,602.6976
  - Fraction of Year5 to recover = 183,602.6976 / 1,186,339.538144 ≈ 0.1547 years
  - Payback ≈ 4 + 0.1547 = 4.1547 years ≈ 4 years 2 months
- Cash-on-cash multiple (5 years) = 3,502,737 / 2,500,000 = 1.4011x
- Approximate annualized return = (1.4011)^(1/5) − 1 ≈ 7.0% per year (rounded)

## Sensitivity notes (where small changes matter)
- Revenue growth: Reducing growth to 10%/yr materially lengthens payback and reduces annualized return.
- COGS%: A 5 percentage point increase in COGS reduces EBITDA and cash flows significantly.
- Fixed costs: If other fixed costs (marketing, platform, rent) are higher than the assumed ₹200k/year, equity returns weaken.
