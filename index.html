Investor Overview

Built for small-town economics, not metro assumptions.

01

Local demand, local supply

Saudafy connects customers with nearby vendors and supports local commerce in towns where distance, trust, and speed matter more than scale.

02

Three revenue streams

The business earns from vendor commissions, delivery fees, and a flat customer platform fee on every order.

03

Lean operations

A small local team, three salaried riders, and one freelance rider create a model designed to keep operating costs controlled in smaller towns.
