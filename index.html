Fixed-Salary Fleet Model (₹7,000/mo)

Unlike metro gig apps that suffer from high attrition and surge pricing, Saudafy provides stable employment with guaranteed base salaries plus performance incentives. Ensures 100% reliability even during off-peak hours.

60-Minute Delivery Guarantee

By partitioning Jhansi like a pizza into 4 distinct operational zones, riders never cross the entire city. Each trip averages 2 to 4 km, executed easily within 20-25 minutes from pickup to doorstep.

Scalable Student & Commercial Targeting

Zone 4 captures massive captive student demand around Bundelkhand University, while Zone 2 and 3 dominate the high-volume commercial markets of Sadar and Sipri Bazaar.
